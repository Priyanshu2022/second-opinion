Privacy Policy — Second Opinion

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Second Opinion collects nothing. There is no server.

What the extension does

When you open a product page on Amazon or Flipkart, the extension reads the product title from that page, turns it into a search query, and asks Reddit's public endpoints what people have said about it. The results are filtered and displayed in a panel on the page.

Data sent off your device

Two destinations, both receiving the same thing: a search query derived from the product title you are already looking at.

  • api.pullpush.io — a community archive of Reddit, used to find which threads
  • reddit.com — the comments themselves are read from Reddit directly, so scores,

Both requests go from your own browser over your own connection. No account, no identifier, and nothing about you accompanies the query.

There is no backend service behind this extension. The developer operates no server, receives no data, and cannot see what you browse or search.

Data stored on your device

Stored locally via chrome.storage.local, and never transmitted:

  • Search results, cached for 7 days so revisiting a product costs no requests.
  • Your settings (whether to check automatically, whether to auto-expand).

You can erase all of it at any time from the extension's options page ("Clear cached results"), or by removing the extension.

What is not collected

No analytics. No telemetry. No crash reporting. No advertising identifiers. No account, no sign-in, no email address. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, because nothing is collected in the first place.

  • Amazon and Flipkart product-page access — to read the product title and draw
  • reddit.com access — to fetch public discussion threads and comments.
  • api.pullpush.io access — to find which threads mention the product, because
  • Storage — to hold the 7-day cache and your settings on this device.

Reddit content

Comments are shown as written, with their subreddit, score, age, and a link back to the original thread. Content is never copied, mirrored, or republished anywhere outside your browser.
